# Audio sources

All audio used in the W2 sample is from Mixkit under its free stock music or
sound-effects license. The production renderer does not fetch remote media.

## Score

- `w2-score.wav`: a 30-second production trim derived from **House Vibez** by
  Lily J. Source: https://assets.mixkit.co/music/745/745.mp3
- `house-vibez.mp3`: the untrimmed source retained for auditability.
